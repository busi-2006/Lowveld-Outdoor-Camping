OUR STORY

From a Garage to the Lowveld

Lowveld Outdoor & Camping Co. is a small retail store based near one of the main gateway towns to the Kruger National Park.

The store was founded eight years ago by Johan Botha, a lifelong hiker and camper.

Johan started by selling second-hand gear from a garage before opening a small shop on the town's main street.

The store now stocks a curated range of tents, sleeping bags, cookware, footwear and backpacks.
