## 1. Project Overview

The Lowveld Outdoor & Camping Co. website is a proposed website
for a small outdoor and camping equipment retailer located near
one of the main gateway towns to the Kruger National Park.

The business specialises in camping, hiking and outdoor equipment,
including tents, sleeping bags, cookware, footwear and backpacks.

The purpose of the website is to provide customers with clear
information about the business and its products while making it
easier for customers to enquire about product availability and
bulk-order requirements.

---

## 2. Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals of the website are:

- To create a professional online presence for the business.
- To provide customers with information about the company.
- To showcase the available outdoor and camping products.
- To allow customers to make product enquiries.
- To provide contact and location information.
- To improve the customer's ability to find relevant information
  before visiting the store.
- To create a clear and easy-to-use navigation structure.

---

## 3. Target Audience

The website is intended for:

- Local residents interested in camping and outdoor activities.
- Tourists visiting the Lowveld region.
- Visitors travelling towards the Kruger National Park.
- Hikers and campers.
- Customers looking for camping equipment.
- Customers interested in bulk orders.

---

## 4. Website Pages

The website contains five main HTML pages:

### Home
Provides an introduction to Lowveld Outdoor & Camping Co.,
featured products and links to important sections of the website.

### About Us
Provides information about the business, its background,
mission and vision.

### Products
Displays the main product categories, including:

- Tents
- Camping cookware
- Outdoor footwear
- Backpacks

### Enquiry
Provides an enquiry form that allows customers to ask about
product availability and bulk-order requirements.

### Contact
Provides contact information, store/location information and
a contact form.
